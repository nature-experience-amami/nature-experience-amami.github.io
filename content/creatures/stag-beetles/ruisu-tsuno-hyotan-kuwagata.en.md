Stag beetle family, subfamily Aesalinae (minute stag beetles), genus Nigidius
(horned gourd stag beetles). *Nigidius lewisi*

Glossy all over the body, with distinctively shaped mandibles, it spends the
whole year inside rotten wood. It can be found year-round in fallen oak and
chinquapin logs and under leaf litter. Telling males and females apart is
difficult. On Amami it has a very localized distribution and rarely comes to
tree sap, making it hard to find.
