Broad-shouldered water strider family, genus Pseudovelia (stream water
striders). *Pseudovelia tsutsuii* Esaki & Miyamoto, 1955

Found in the Ryukyu Islands (from Takarajima to Okinawa Island). Several
species of the genus Pseudovelia are known in Japan, a group that lives along
the banks of rivers and ponds. Sufficient information on the detailed ecology
of this species itself has not yet been confirmed.
