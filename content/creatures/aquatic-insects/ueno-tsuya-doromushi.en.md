Riffle beetle family, genus Urumaelmis. *Urumaelmis uenoi uenoi* (Nomura, 1961)

A small aquatic beetle found from Yakushima through the Amami
island group to the Okinawa island group (nominate subspecies). The genus Urumaelmis also includes a
second species newly described from Kyushu in 2020, bringing the number of
known species in Japan to two. The population on Kuchinoshima in the Tokara
Islands is distinguished as a separate subspecies (*U. u. tokarana*). The detailed ecology of
this species itself is still largely unknown.
