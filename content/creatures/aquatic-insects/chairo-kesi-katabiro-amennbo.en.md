Broad-shouldered water strider family, genus Microvelia. *Microvelia japonica*
Esaki & Miyamoto, 1955

A small aquatic insect about 1.5-2.1mm long. Found on Honshu (west of
Fukushima Prefecture), Shikoku, and Kyushu, and in the Ryukyu Islands from
Kikaijima to Okinawa Island. Commonly seen in dim pools beside small streams
and in the shade of rocks, where it preys on small insects that fall onto the
water's surface.
