Water scavenger beetle family, genus Laccobius. *Laccobius satoi* Gentili, 1989

A small water scavenger beetle endemic to Japan, found in Amami Oshima and Tokunoshima. Several closely related species exist within the genus Laccobius in Japan, including L. fragilis and L. nakanei, and telling them apart requires examining the genitalia. Detailed records of its body length and ecology are scarce, and much remains unknown.
