Water scavenger beetle family, genus Laccobius. *Laccobius (Microlaccobius) satoi* Gentili, 1989

A small water scavenger beetle currently known only from Amami Oshima and Tokunoshima. Several closely related species exist within the Japanese subgenus Microlaccobius of Laccobius, including L. fragilis and L. nakanei, and telling them apart requires examining the genitalia. Detailed records of its body length and ecology are scarce, and much remains unknown.
