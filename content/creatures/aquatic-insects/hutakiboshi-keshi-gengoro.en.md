Diving beetle family, genus Allopachria. *Allopachria bimaculata* (M. Satô, 1972)

A tiny diving beetle said to bear two spots on its elytra, as its species name (meaning "two-spotted") suggests. It is said to be distinguished from closely related species by its smaller body and the incomplete microreticulation on its head. Primary sources on its detailed ecology, morphology, and the extent of its distribution are scarce, but it is listed as Near Threatened (NT) on the Ministry of the Environment's insect Red List.
