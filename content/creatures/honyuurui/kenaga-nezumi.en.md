Rat family (Muridae), genus Diplothrix. *Diplothrix legata*

Body length 22-33 cm, tail length 25-35 cm. Japan's largest rat, found only
on Amami Oshima, Tokunoshima, and the northern part of Okinawa Island, with
long bristle-like hairs of 5-6 cm mixed into its coat. Designated a Natural
Monument in 1972 and a Nationally Rare Wild Species in 2016. Nocturnal and
strongly arboreal, it rests during the day in tree-hollow nests lined with
dead leaves. Omnivorous, feeding on chinquapin nuts and insects among other
things. Breeds from autumn into early winter, giving birth to 2-5 young per
litter. It is not very wary, and since it does not flee much even when a
flashlight is shone on it, it can sometimes be observed at leisure along
forest roads.
