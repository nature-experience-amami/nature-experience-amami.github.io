Shrew family (Soricidae), genus Crocidura. *Crocidura watasei*

One of the world's smallest mammals, about 5.5 cm long and weighing just 5-8
g. A species endemic to Japan, found only in the Amami island group (Amami
Oshima, Kakeromajima, Tokunoshima, and others) and the Okinawa Islands. It
prefers open environments such as grassland and farmland, and feeds on small
insects and spiders. Nocturnal, it can sometimes be seen darting quickly
through grassy patches beside forest roads. It differs in habitat from a
forest-dwelling relative of the same genus, and the two are sometimes
confused. It carries no legal capture restriction like a Natural Monument
designation, but it is listed as Near Threatened by the Ministry of the
Environment.
