Old World flycatcher family (Muscicapidae), genus Larvivora.
*Larvivora komadori*

About 14 cm long, weighing about 18 g. A species endemic to Japan that
breeds from the Danjo Islands through the Tokara Islands, Amami Oshima and
its surrounding islets, to Tokunoshima; designated a Natural Monument in
1970. Males have a vivid orange-red head, back, and tail, and the black
patches on the breast and flanks look like a "beard," which gives the
species its Japanese name. Females are overall plainer in color. It forages
for insects on the forest floor of evergreen broadleaf forest, proclaiming
its territory with a strong, beautiful song. It is easiest to find early in
the morning by following its song. A different species lives in northern
Okinawa Island, but the two ranges do not overlap.
