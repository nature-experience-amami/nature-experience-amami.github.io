Pigeon family (Columbidae), genus Treron. *Treron permagnus*

About 35 cm long, somewhat larger than the White-bellied Green-Pigeon, and
a year-round resident. Its whole body is a vivid green. The Japanese name,
meaning "red-headed green-pigeon," comes from the reddish head of its
close relative the Taiwan Whistling Green-Pigeon (Treron formosae), but
this species, found on Amami Oshima and elsewhere, is green even on the
head. It lives in laurel forest from lowlands to mountains and feeds in
the trees on fruit and seeds, including that of Ficus erecta. It gives a
distinctive shakuhachi-like call, "oo-ao, pe, hepo." It breeds around May,
laying two eggs. Due to habitat fragmentation and decline, it is listed as
Near Threatened on the IUCN Red List.
