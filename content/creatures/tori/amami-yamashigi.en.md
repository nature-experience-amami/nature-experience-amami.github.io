Sandpiper family (Scolopacidae), genus Scolopax. *Scolopax mira*

About 35-36 cm long. A species endemic to Japan found only in the Amami and
Okinawa island groups, with breeding confirmed on Amami Oshima, Kakeromajima,
Uke-jima, Yoro-jima, and Tokunoshima. It resembles the closely related
Eurasian woodcock but has longer legs, an overall blacker tone, and a
stockier build than other sandpipers. It prefers the dim forest floor of
evergreen broadleaf forest, is most active from dusk to dawn, and forages on
the ground for earthworms and insects. It becomes especially active in the
dim light of early morning and evening, and on moonlit nights. It nests on
the ground within forests and lays 2-4 eggs from around March through May.
Designated a Nationally Rare Wild Species in 1993. Predation by mongooses
and feral cats, along with habitat loss from logging, are ongoing concerns.
