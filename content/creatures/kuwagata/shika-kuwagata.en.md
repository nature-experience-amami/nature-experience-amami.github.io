Stag beetle family, genus Rhaetulus (deer stag beetles). *Rhaetulus recticrnis*

Active from around May through September, most easily seen around August.
Collecting is prohibited by ordinance. Areas of relatively higher elevation
offer a better chance of finding it. It is often found on thin branches of
Castanopsis sieboldii or citrus trees. In large individuals the mandibles curve
strongly, like a deer's antlers. Take care not to mistake it for a small
individual of the lesser stag beetle or sawtooth stag beetle and take it home
by accident (never take home an individual you can't identify yourself).
