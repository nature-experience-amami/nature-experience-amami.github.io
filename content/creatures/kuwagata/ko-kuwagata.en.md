Stag beetle family, subgenus Dorcus (lesser stag beetles). *Dorcus amamianus*

Although its name means "lesser stag beetle," it resembles the Himeoo stag
beetle in many respects. Its mandibles are short and strongly curved, while its
legs and tarsi are long. Active from late May through October. Less numerous
than the lesser stag beetle found on mainland Japan.
