Stag beetle family, genus Aegus (root stag beetles). *Aegus laevicolis subnitidus*

A subspecies of the root stag beetle found in the Amami Islands, and the
largest-growing of Japan's root stag beetle subspecies. Active from around May
through October. It sometimes comes to the sap of machilus, chinquapin,
mallotus, and Albizia trees. In the wild most individuals are around 20mm, and
28mm is considered large. Aim for 30mm+.
