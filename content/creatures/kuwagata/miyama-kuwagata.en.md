Stag beetle family, genus Lucanus (true stag beetles). *Lucanus ferriei*

Active from around July through September. A rare species found only on Amami
Oshima. Collecting is prohibited by ordinance. Lives in primeval forest above
about 300m elevation. Nocturnal; males can be seen waiting for females high in
trees, but they are hard to spot visually. They are also often seen near the
tops of utility poles. They come to tree sap, fruit traps, and lights, but
collecting them is prohibited.
