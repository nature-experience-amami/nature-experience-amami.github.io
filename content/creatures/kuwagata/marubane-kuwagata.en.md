Stag beetle family, genus Neolucanus (roundwing stag beetles).
*Neolucanus protogenetivus*

Active from around September through October, it can be observed deep in
forests dense with giant Castanopsis sieboldii trees. Collecting is prohibited
under the rare wildlife protection ordinances of five municipalities on Amami
Oshima (including its outlying islands) and three towns on Tokunoshima. To
prevent poaching, cameras have been set up in the mountains, and insect patrols
make rounds during the season. Note: simply taking photographs is fine, but if
you need to enter an area that requires a permit, be sure to apply properly.
