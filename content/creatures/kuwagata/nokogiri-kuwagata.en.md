Stag beetle family, genus Prosopocoilus (sawtooth stag beetles); Amami
subspecies. *Prosopocoilus dissimilis*

Active from mid-June through early September. Among Japan's sawtooth stag
beetles, those from Amami Oshima are especially large, with long, thick,
imposing mandibles. It frequently flies to citrus trees, mallotus, machilus,
chinquapin, ripe Pandanus fruit and banana traps, and streetlights. Most
individuals are around 60mm, and anything over 75mm is large. Aim for 80mm.
