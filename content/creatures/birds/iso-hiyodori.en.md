Old World flycatcher family (Muscicapidae), genus Monticola. *Monticola
solitarius*

About 23 cm long. Males and females look very different: males are blue from
the head to the back with reddish-brown underparts, while females are
grayish-brown with dark scaly barring. A year-round resident on Amami
Oshima, it traditionally favors rocky coastlines and harbors — as its
Japanese name ("rocky shore thrush") suggests — but in recent years it has
been spreading inland and into towns across Japan, and on Amami it is
increasingly seen around houses, on rooftops, power lines, and in parking
lots. A strongly predatory bird, it feeds on crustaceans, insects, lizards,
and even small rodents, and sings a high, clear, elaborate song. It is
solitary and holds a year-round territory; breeding is thought to begin
around March.
