Hawk family (Accipitridae), genus Tachyspiza. *Tachyspiza soloensis* (until a
2024 taxonomic revision, classified as *Accipiter soloensis*)

A small hawk about 30 cm long that does not breed in Japan, passing through
only in spring and autumn as a migrant. The upperparts are dark bluish-gray
and the underparts pale orange-brown, with reddish-brown eyes in males and
yellow eyes in females. It breeds from northeastern China through the Korean
Peninsula and winters in Southeast Asia. Spring passage also occurs, but
Amami Oshima sees its largest numbers in autumn, when the passage peaks from
mid-September through October as cicadas and dragonflies that serve as prey
become abundant; flocks can sometimes be seen circling upward together on
rising air currents in what is called a "hawk pillar." It preys widely on
small animals such as frogs, insects, and lizards. It is listed as Least
Concern on the IUCN Red List and is not subject to designation under Japan's
Ministry of the Environment Red List or Act on Conservation of Endangered
Species.
