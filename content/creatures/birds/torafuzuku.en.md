Owl family (Strigidae), genus Asio. *Asio otus*

About 35-40 cm long, an owl notable for the long, ear-like tufts of
feathers on its head. The upperparts are grayish to pale brown with
streaking, and the eyes are orange. It breeds as a summer visitor in
Hokkaido and northern Honshu, but from central Honshu southward it occurs
mainly as a winter visitor. The only owls resident year-round on Amami
Oshima are the Ryukyu Scops Owl and the Ryukyu Brown Hawk-Owl; aside from
these, a small number of Long-eared Owls arrive as winter visitors each
year, typically appearing from around November. Outside the breeding
season it is known to form communal roosts, gathering in groups in
evergreen trees or bamboo thickets. It preys mainly on small mammals such
as rodents, as well as insects and small birds. Few individuals reach Amami
Oshima, and because they are quite wary, they can be difficult to observe.
