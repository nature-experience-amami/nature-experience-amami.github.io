Stilt family (Recurvirostridae), genus Himantopus. *Himantopus himantopus*

About 37-40 cm long, a shorebird with extremely long, reddish legs and a
thin black bill. The body is white with black wings and back; males show
more black extending to the back of the head, while females are paler. It
feeds on aquatic insects, crustaceans, shellfish, and small fish. In Japan
it was first confirmed breeding at reclaimed land in Aichi Prefecture in
1975 and has since become locally established in parts of Honshu, but in
the Nansei Islands it occurs mainly as a passage migrant and winter
visitor. Within Kagoshima Prefecture it has been recorded on Amami Oshima,
Tokunoshima, and Nakanoshima in the Tokara Islands, and breeding was
confirmed on nearby Okinoerabujima in 2009, though there are few records of
breeding on Amami Oshima itself, or of exactly when it arrives there. It
favors shallow water such as tidal flats, rice paddies, and fallow fields.
It was previously classified as Vulnerable (VU) on the Ministry of the
Environment Red List but is now listed as Data Deficient (DD).
