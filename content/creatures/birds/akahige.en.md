Old World flycatcher family (Muscicapidae), genus Larvivora.
*Larvivora komadori*

About 14 cm long, weighing about 18 g. A species endemic to Japan that
breeds from the Danjo Islands through the Tokara Islands, Amami Oshima and
its surrounding islets, to Tokunoshima; designated a Natural Monument in
1970. It is a year-round resident on Amami Oshima, but males sing especially
often to proclaim territory during the breeding season from March to June,
so following the song early in the morning is the best way to find one at
that time of year. Males have a vivid orange-red head, back, and tail, and
the black patches on the breast and flanks look like a "beard," which gives
the species its Japanese name. Females are overall plainer in color. It
forages for insects on the forest floor of evergreen broadleaf forest. A
different species lives in northern Okinawa Island, but the two ranges do
not overlap.
