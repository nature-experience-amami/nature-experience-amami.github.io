Old World flycatcher family (Muscicapidae), genus Zoothera.
*Zoothera dauma major*

About 30 cm long. A resident bird found only on Amami Oshima, with a
yellowish-brown ground color marked with black, tiger-like blotches.
Designated a national Natural Monument and a Nationally Rare Wild Species.
It prefers the dim forest floor of mature broadleaf forest and feeds on
earthworms and the like. During the breeding season from February to early
April, it sings a clear, beautiful "kyoroon, tsi-i" call in the dim light
before dawn and at dusk. It once declined so sharply due to logging and the
impact of invasive species that it was called a "phantom bird," but as the
forest has recovered, its population has been on a recovering trend in
recent years.
