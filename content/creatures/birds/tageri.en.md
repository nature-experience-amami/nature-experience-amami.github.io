Plover family (Charadriidae), genus Vanellus. *Vanellus vanellus*

About 32 cm long, with a long crest on the head, a back with a glossy
greenish-black sheen, and a white belly. Its elegant appearance has earned
it the nickname "winter lady." It breeds across Eurasia and visits Japan
as a winter migrant. On Amami Oshima it begins to appear in open farmland
such as rice paddies and fields from around late October, staying through
the winter until around late March or April of the following year. It
moves in flocks, foraging in the soil for earthworms and insects. Unlike
the creatures of the night forest that this site mainly features, it is a
messenger of winter that is easy to observe by day in open farmland.
