Riffle beetle family, genus Dryopomorphus. *Dryopomorphus amami*

An endemic species found only on Amami Oshima, described in 2005. The genus
Dryopomorphus also includes a widespread relative found on Honshu, Shikoku,
and Kyushu along with a smaller species, plus another species endemic to the
Osumi Islands. Riffle beetles are mainly small aquatic beetles living in clean
streams, but the detailed ecology of this species itself is still largely
unknown.
