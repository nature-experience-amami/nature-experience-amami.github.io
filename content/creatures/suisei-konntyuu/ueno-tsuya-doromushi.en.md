Riffle beetle family, genus Urumaelmis. *Urumaelmis uenoi*

A small aquatic beetle found in the Osumi Islands, Tokara Islands, Amami
island group, and Okinawa island group. The genus Urumaelmis also includes a
second species newly described from Kyushu in 2020, bringing the number of
known species in Japan to two. The population on Kuchinoshima in the Tokara
Islands is distinguished as a separate subspecies. The detailed ecology of
this species itself is still largely unknown.
