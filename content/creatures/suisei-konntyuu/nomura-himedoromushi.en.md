Riffle beetle family, genus Stenelmis. *Stenelmis amami* Yoshitomi et Hayashi, 2021

A small aquatic beetle found in the Amami and Okinawa island groups. Named
after Dr. Osamu Nomura, a pioneer of riffle beetle research in Japan. The
detailed ecology of this species itself is still largely unknown.
