Riffle beetle family, genus Stenelmis. *Stenelmis amami* Yoshitomi et Hayashi, 2021

A small aquatic beetle found in the Amami and Okinawa island groups. It was
originally treated as the sole species of its own genus, but a 2021 molecular
phylogenetic study (Kobayashi et al., 2021) placed it in the genus Stenelmis.
Named after Dr. Osamu Nomura, a pioneer of riffle beetle research in Japan.
The detailed ecology of this species itself is still largely unknown.
