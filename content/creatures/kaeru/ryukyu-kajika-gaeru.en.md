Tree frog family, genus Buergeria. *Buergeria japonica*

Grows to about 2.5-4 cm. Breeds from April through October. Adaptable to
habitats ranging from mountain streams to puddles in built-up areas, it is the
most commonly seen frog on Amami Oshima. It calls especially vigorously on
humid or rainy days. Males are a beautiful yellow, and their call is beautiful
too.
