Brown frog family, genus Odorrana. *Odorrana amamiensis*

Grows to about 5.6-10.1 cm. Breeds from October through May. Lives around
mountain streams. Slender with good posture, and once you get an eye for it, it
can be recognized even from a distance. Its coloring is rich and varies between
individuals. Designated a Natural Monument in Kagoshima Prefecture, so capturing
or transferring it — including its eggs — is prohibited. Many come out onto
forest roads when it rains, so drive carefully.
