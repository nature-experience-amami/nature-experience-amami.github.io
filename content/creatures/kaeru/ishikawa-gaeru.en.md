Brown frog family, genus Odorrana. *Odorrana splendida*

Grows to about 7.4-13.7 cm. Breeds from late February through around June. Lives
along the gentle headwater streams of mountain areas. Said to be Japan's most
beautiful frog, it is designated a Natural Monument and a Nationally Rare Wild
Species in Kagoshima Prefecture, and capturing or transferring it — including
its eggs — is prohibited. In some spots near higher-elevation streams, its call
can be heard after dark, and at certain locations it can even be seen in summer
when it rains.
