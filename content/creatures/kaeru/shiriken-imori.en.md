Newt family, genus Cynops. *Cynops ensicauda ensicauda*

Males reach about 14 cm and females about 18 cm in total length. Found on Amami
Oshima, Uke-jima, Kakeromajima, and Yoro-jima, it breeds in ponds, rice paddies,
and gentle stretches of streams. After September most individuals leave the
water, and around December, during the breeding season, large numbers can be
observed at the water's edge. It has a black body with yellow or orange spots,
and individual variation is considerable. Okinawa is home to the related
Okinawa sword-tailed newt.
