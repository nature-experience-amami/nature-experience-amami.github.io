Tree frog family (Rhacophoridae), genus Zhangixalus. *Zhangixalus amamiensis*

Grows to about 4.5-7.7 cm. Breeds from late December through around May. Found
from lowlands to mountains, in forests around ponds, rice paddies, and other still
water. It calls vigorously on humid or rainy days and lays white, foam-covered egg
masses on branches overhead or in streamside vegetation. Even when calling loudly,
it is very hard to spot. Larger than Hallowell's tree frog, which makes the two
easier to tell apart.
