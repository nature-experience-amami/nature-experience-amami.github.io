Brown frog family, genus Rana. *Rana kobai*

Grows to about 3.2-4.6 cm. Breeds from November through January. Lives in forests
and along rivers from lowlands to mountains. Becomes active on humid or rainy days,
and gathers around small ponds and spring-fed pools during the breeding season.
Individuals can also be seen along forest roads. Slender-bodied and reddish brown,
it calls with a sharp "kyok-kyok" note.
