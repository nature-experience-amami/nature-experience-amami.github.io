Brown frog family, genus Babina. *Babina subaspera*

Grows to about 9-14 cm. Breeds from April through October. Lives mainly in
mountainous areas, but can also be seen in farmland and parks. It becomes active
on humid or rainy days and often comes out onto roads. Males grow larger, making
this Japan's biggest frog aside from the introduced American bullfrog. It has
five toes with a sharp spur on what corresponds to the thumb, thought to be used
by males when fighting each other or during amplexus. Designated a Natural
Monument and a Nationally Rare Wild Species in Kagoshima Prefecture, capturing
or transferring it — including its eggs — is prohibited. It is very large and
impressive, and easy to spot when it comes out onto a road. Its name is said to
come from its call.
