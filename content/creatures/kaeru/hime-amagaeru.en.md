Narrow-mouthed frog family, genus Microhyla. *Microhyla okinavensis*

Grows to about 2.2-3.2 cm. Breeds year-round (mainly March through July). Found
in a wide range of habitats from coastal lowlands to mountains, including forest
edges, grasslands, and around houses. Becomes active on humid or rainy days. Its
head is small and its legs are thick, giving it a triangular shape when viewed
from above. Japan's smallest frog, but it calls with a surprisingly loud voice.
