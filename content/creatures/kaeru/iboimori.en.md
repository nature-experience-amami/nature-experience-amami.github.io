Newt family (Salamandridae), genus Echinotriton. *Echinotriton raffaellii*

Grows to about 14-20 cm in total length. Breeds from around January through
April. Endemic to Amami Oshima, Uke-jima, and Tokunoshima, it lives around
forests and farmland. It becomes active on warm winter days, and during the
breeding season lays eggs under fallen leaves near water. It has a rugged,
blackish-brown body, with orange tints on its rib-tip projections and the ends
of its limbs.
