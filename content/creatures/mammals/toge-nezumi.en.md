Rat family, genus Tokudaia (spiny rats). *Tokudaia osimensis*

Endemic to Amami Oshima, designated a national Natural Monument in 1972.
Body length 8.9-16 cm. The fur on its back is spine-like (only slightly
stiffer than ordinary hair). A different species from the spiny rats of
Okinawa Island and Tokunoshima. Omnivorous, feeding on Castanopsis sieboldii
nuts, sweet potato, ants, and more. Its predator is the Habu pit viper.
Breeds from October through December, with 1-7 young per litter. Nocturnal,
and individuals are often observed running about on forest roads.
Distinctively, it curls up and points its tail upward while bounding around
a lot (unlike the black rat, which stretches its belly low and scurries
along).
