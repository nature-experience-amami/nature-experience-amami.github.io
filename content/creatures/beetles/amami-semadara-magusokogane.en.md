Scarabaeidae, subfamily Aphodiinae, Amami Mottled Dung Beetle *Chilothorax ohishii* (Masumoto, 1975)

An endemic species found only on Amami Oshima and Tokunoshima. It was described in 1975 by Toshiya Masumoto as *Aphodius ohishii*, but is now placed in the genus Chilothorax. It feeds mainly on the droppings of the Amami rabbit, and is commonly seen from autumn through spring, while rarely appearing in summer.
