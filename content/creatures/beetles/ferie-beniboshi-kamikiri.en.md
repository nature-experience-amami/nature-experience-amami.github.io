Cerambycidae, Ferrié's Red Longhorn Beetle *Rosalia (Eurybatus) ferriei* Vuillet, 1911

An endemic species found only on Amami Oshima. It has a beautiful red body marked with black patterns, and appears on standing dead trees deep in the forest around July. It is named after Father Ferrié, who collected the species on Amami Oshima. It is designated a Rare Wild Species under Kagoshima Prefecture's ordinance for the protection of rare wildlife, and its capture or collection is prohibited.
