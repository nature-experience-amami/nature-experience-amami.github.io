Cantharidae, Amami Blue Soldier Beetle *Themus kazuoi*

Body length just under 15mm. A soldier beetle with a blue metallic sheen, distributed on Amami Oshima, Tokunoshima, and Okinawa Island (the Okinawa Island population is treated as a separate subspecies). Adults appear in spring and can be seen on flowers around the time the chinquapin (Castanopsis) trees bloom. Soldier beetles are carnivorous and catch and eat small insects.
