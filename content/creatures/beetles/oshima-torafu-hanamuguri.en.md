Scarabaeidae, Oshima Tiger Flower Chafer *Paratrichius duplicatus duplicatus* Lewis, 1895

Body length 11.8–15.6mm. The nominate subspecies (P. d. duplicatus), found on Amami Oshima and Tokunoshima; a different subspecies, the Okinawa Tiger Flower Chafer (P. d. okinawanus), occurs in the Okinawa island group. Compared with related tiger flower chafers, its wing covers are tinged darker, giving it a distinctive sheen. It appears from April to May.
