Camel cricket family (Rhaphidophoridae), Amami Spotted Camel Cricket *Diestrammena gigas*

A spotted camel cricket found on Amami Oshima and Tokunoshima. It is larger than the mainland spotted camel cricket (body length 20-34mm), and its mottled pattern is indistinct. It has no wings, and has long antennae and well-developed hind legs. Rather than walking around, it usually moves in small hops. It can be seen in the forest at night, and adults appear from summer to autumn.
