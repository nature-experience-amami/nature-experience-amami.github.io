Owlet moth family (Erebidae in recent classification) Great Ring-spot Moth *Erebus ephesperis* (Hübner, [1823])

The largest of the tomoe moths, with a wingspan reaching 90–100mm. Its brown wings have a wavy, undulating edge and a ring-shaped white marking on the forewing — the source of its Japanese name, meaning "great comma." The larvae feed on Smilax (catbrier) plants, and the adults also come to lights. On Amami Oshima, it has also been documented academically as a pollinator of Hoya (wax vine) flowers.
