Bush cricket family (Eneopteridae) Rotten Wood Cricket *Duolandrevus ivani*

A somewhat large cricket-like insect, 31–35mm long. Widely distributed across the Nansei Islands, including Amami Oshima and Tokunoshima, it lives hidden beneath the bark of rotting wood, as its Japanese name suggests. At night it calls with a distinctive "gwiii, gwiii." In warm regions, adults can be seen year-round regardless of season.
