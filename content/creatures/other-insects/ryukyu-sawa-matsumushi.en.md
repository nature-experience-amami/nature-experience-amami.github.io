Phalangopsid cricket family (Phalangopsidae), Ryukyu Stream Cricket *Vescelia pieli ryukyuensis* (Oshiro, 1985)

Length to the wing tips is about 18mm in males and about 26mm in females. It is distributed in the Nansei Islands, from Amami Oshima and Tokunoshima to Okinawa Island, Kumejima, Ishigaki Island and Iriomote Island. It lives along mountain streams, hiding in places such as rock crevices by day, and at night it comes out onto fern leaves and similar spots to sing. In appearance it looks like something between a bell cricket (suzumushi) and a pine cricket (matsumushi). Only the males sing, with a clear, far-carrying voice. Because they turn their bodies while singing, it is hard to tell where the sound is coming from. Adults can be seen from spring to winter, but their numbers rise and fall greatly depending on the year and season. It was described in 1985 as a new species of the genus *Phaloria*, and is now treated as a subspecies of *Vescelia pieli*.
