Broad-winged damselfly family (Calopterygidae), Ryukyu Black-winged Damselfly *Matrona japonica*

Body length about 65mm. Distributed on Amami Oshima, Tokunoshima, and Okinawa Island, it lives along the upper to middle reaches of dim mountain streams surrounded by forest. Its body has a blue-green metallic sheen, and its black wings shine blue depending on the light. On Amami Oshima individuals, the blue-shining area of the wings is wider than on those from Okinawa Island. Adults can be seen over a long period, from late February to around December.
