Skink family (Scincidae), genus Ateuchosaurus. *Ateuchosaurus pellopleurus*

Total length 9-12 cm (snout-vent length 4.2-6.9 cm). A species endemic to Japan, distributed on Takeshima, Iojima, and Kuroshima in the Osumi Islands, the Tokara Islands, and the Amami Islands. A small skink with a long body and short legs; the tail makes up about half of its total length. It lives on the damp forest floor, moving quickly among fallen leaves and the gaps between stones. It is most often seen in the daytime on sunny days from spring to autumn. It was formerly known in Japanese as Heriguro-hime-tokage.
