Agamid family (Agamidae), genus Diploderma. *Diploderma polygonatum polygonatum*

Total length 20-30 cm (up to 31 cm in males and 24 cm in females). A subspecies endemic to Japan, distributed in the Amami and Okinawa Islands. A large lizard with a greenish body; as its name suggests, it lives in trees, and males hold territories. It prefers fairly open environments such as the edges of broadleaf forest, secondary forest, and trees in parks and gardens, catching insects and other prey in the daytime. At night it sleeps on branches and leaves, and on night tours you may be able to see it asleep. Its numbers have declined due to logging, predation by mongooses and other animals, and collection for the pet trade, and it is listed as Vulnerable on the Ministry of the Environment's Red List.
