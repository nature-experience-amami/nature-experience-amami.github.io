Skink family (Scincidae), genus Plestiodon. *Plestiodon barbouri*

Total length 15.5-20 cm (snout-vent length 5.2-7.3 cm). A species endemic to
Japan, distributed on Amami Oshima, Kakeromajima, Uke-jima, Yoro-jima,
Tokunoshima, and the Okinawa Islands. Juveniles are blackish brown with five
bright longitudinal stripes, and a vivid blue tail that makes up more than
two-thirds of the total tail length. As they grow, the blue fades; males
become overall dark brown to brown, while many females retain coloring
closer to that of juveniles. It prefers the forest floor and edges of
natural evergreen broadleaf forest or well-recovered secondary forest,
basking in the sun to regulate its body temperature while feeding on
spiders and land snails. It becomes sluggish in winter but does not
hibernate. Its numbers have declined due to drying of habitat from logging
and undergrowth clearance, and predation by feral cats and the introduced
small Indian mongoose. It is listed as Vulnerable on the Ministry of the
Environment's Red List, and in 2019 was also designated a Kagoshima
Prefecture Rare Wildlife Species, making unauthorized capture or collection
subject to penalties.
