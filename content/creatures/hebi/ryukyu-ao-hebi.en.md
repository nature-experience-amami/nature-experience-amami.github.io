Colubrid snake family, genus Cycophuops. *Cycophuops semicarinatus*

Grows up to about 100 cm. Most easily observed from May through November. Mainly
diurnal, though many individuals are also active at night. It has a small head and feeds
mainly on earthworms, as well as frogs. It is the most numerous snake on Amami Oshima and
can be seen in forests during the day and along forest roads at night. Larger individuals
will sometimes threaten you, but they flee very quickly, sliding down slopes to escape.
