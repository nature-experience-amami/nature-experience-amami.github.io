Cobra family, genus Sinomicrurus (also known as the Ryukyu coral snake). *Sinomicrurus
japonicus*

Grows up to about 50 cm. Most easily observed around May and around September. The back
is reddish-brown or orange with five dark brown longitudinal stripes. It is designated a
"Specified Dangerous Animal," a legal category for species considered capable of harming
people. Its venom is neurotoxic and stronger than the Habu's, but its head is small and
its temperament is docile. If picked up, it will jab you with its tail, which is as hard
as bone. It is nocturnal and scarce, so sightings are rare. It is popular enough that some
people visit Amami Oshima specifically hoping to see one.
