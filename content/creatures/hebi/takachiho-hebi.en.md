Takachiho snake family, Takachiho tribe. *Achalinus werneri*

Grows up to about 55 cm. The back is blackish brown or dark brown with a black stripe
running along the midline; the belly is yellow. On Amami it is a legendary snake that you
only encounter under just the right conditions. It sometimes comes to the surface on days
of heavy rain. Nocturnal and subterranean, it lives in forests, grassy areas near water,
and farmland. It feeds on earthworms. It is scarcer and harder to find here than the
Amami odd-scaled snakes on other islands. It is very slender and small, so you may not
notice it even if you drive right past.
