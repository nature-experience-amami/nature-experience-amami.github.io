Colubrid snake family, genus Dinodon. *Dinodon semicarinatum*

Grows up to about 200 cm. Most easily observed from May through November. The body is
yellowish brown with red-and-black oval crossbands. It has no venom but is aggressive,
hissing and striking if you enter its territory. It feeds on mammals, birds, reptiles,
amphibians, and fish, and even eats sea turtle eggs; it sometimes preys on its natural
enemy, the Habu pit viper. Nocturnal, it can be seen along forest roads and around water,
all the way to the coast. Smaller individuals show stronger red coloring and are very
beautiful, but even a small one gives a painful bite, so take care.
