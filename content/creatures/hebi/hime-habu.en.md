Pit viper family, genus Ovophis. *Ovophis okinavensis*

Grows up to about 80 cm. Cold-tolerant, and on Amami Oshima it can be observed
year-round. Once the rainy season ends and the weather turns hot, it is often seen at
higher elevations. It feeds on small to medium-sized frogs, small mammals, and birds. It
is commonly observed in places with plenty of frogs, such as rice paddies and
slow-flowing streams. A coiled individual barely moves, but it will threaten you if you
enter its territory. It is a venomous snake, so if bitten, go to a hospital immediately.
Be careful not to step on it.
