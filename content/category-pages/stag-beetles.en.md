A variety of stag beetles appear in Amami Oshima's forests, mainly on summer nights. You may spot their powerful forms on sap-flowing trees or along forest roads.

Some have large mandibles, others are small-bodied and hide in tree bark — each species' form and way of life differs. Some are precious species found only on Amami.

If you meet one in the forest, quietly watch it feed on sap and move about, and enjoy capturing its life in the wild in a photograph.
